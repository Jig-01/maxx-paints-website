Colours

Give Your Walls a Fresh New Personality

How to choose a wall colour direction that feels right for the room, the light and the way you use the space.

MAXX Paints•Paint & Surface Guide

Colour can change the way a room feels before you add a single piece of furniture. The right choice depends on more than a colour card: consider natural light, room size, existing finishes and the mood you want to create.

Colour is one of the simplest ways to change the character of a room, but the right choice depends on more than a colour card. Light, room size, existing décor and the mood you want all influence how a shade will look once it is actually on the wall.

Share this guide
01

Look at the light first

A colour can appear different in bright daylight, soft evening light and artificial lighting. Observe your shortlisted shades at different times before making a final decision.

02

Think about the room's purpose

Bedrooms may suit calm, comfortable colour directions, while living rooms can handle warmer or more expressive combinations. Work spaces often benefit from colours that feel clear and balanced.

03

Create a simple palette

Instead of choosing every wall independently, start with one main wall colour and build supporting shades around it. This creates a more connected look across the home.

04

Use contrast intentionally

A darker accent wall, a lighter ceiling or a subtle contrast around architectural details can add depth without making the room feel busy.

05

Test before committing

Whenever possible, view a sample on the actual wall. The surrounding furniture, flooring and daylight can change the way a colour reads.

MAXX PRO TIP

Preparation makes the finish.

If you are unsure, start with the largest visual elements already in the room and choose a wall palette that complements them rather than competing with them.

QUICK SUMMARY

Key takeaways

  • Check colour in real lighting.
  • Match the palette to the room's purpose.
  • Keep the overall home visually connected.
  • Test shortlisted shades before painting the full room.
