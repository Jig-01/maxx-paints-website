Getting Your Walls Ready for a Fresh Coat

A straightforward wall-preparation checklist covering cleaning, repairs, priming and the final checks before painting.

MAXX Paints•Paint & Surface Guide

Good preparation does not have to be complicated. A simple sequence of checks can help you avoid common problems and give your finishing coat a cleaner surface to work with.

Before opening the paint can, spend a little time understanding what the wall needs. Preparation is where many of the decisions that influence the final appearance are made, from repairing imperfections to creating a clean and suitable base.

Share this guide
01

Inspect the wall

Look for cracks, holes, damp-looking areas, peeling material and stains. Identify the problem before starting the cosmetic part of the job.

02

Clean thoroughly

Remove dust, loose particles and other surface contamination. The wall should be clean and appropriately dry for the next stage.

03

Repair and smooth

Fill or repair imperfections using a suitable wall-preparation material. Once dry, smooth the repaired areas so they blend into the surrounding surface.

04

Prime where required

A suitable primer can help prepare the surface for the finishing coat. Apply it evenly and allow it to dry according to its instructions.

05

Do a final check

Run a final visual inspection before opening the paint. Small corrections are much easier to make before the finishing coat goes on.

MAXX PRO TIP

Preparation makes the finish.

Use a strong light across the wall during your final inspection. Side lighting can reveal small ridges, scratches and uneven patches that normal room lighting may hide.

QUICK SUMMARY

Key takeaways

  • Inspect before you begin.
  • Clean and repair carefully.
  • Smooth repaired areas.
  • Prime where appropriate.
  • Check the surface before the final coat.
