Small Changes That Can Transform a Room

Simple wall-colour and finishing ideas that can refresh a room without changing everything inside it.

MAXX Paints•Paint & Surface Guide

A room does not always need a complete makeover. A thoughtful change to the walls can alter the visual balance of a space while keeping the furniture and layout largely the same.

Refreshing a room does not always require replacing everything in it. A thoughtful change in colour, a stronger focal point or better coordination between walls and existing décor can make the space feel noticeably different while keeping the overall project manageable.

Share this guide
01

Refresh one feature wall

Choose a wall that naturally draws attention and give it a new colour direction. This can create a focal point without repainting every surface.

02

Make a small room feel calmer

Lighter, coordinated shades can help a compact room feel visually open. Keep the palette simple and use contrast through furniture or accessories.

03

Use ceilings as part of the palette

The ceiling does not have to be an afterthought. A clean, light finish can support the overall colour scheme and make the room feel more complete.

04

Connect adjoining spaces

When two rooms are visible from one another, related colours can create continuity. You can vary the shade while keeping the underlying colour family consistent.

05

Let details do the work

You do not need many colours to create interest. A carefully selected accent, clean edges and a well-prepared wall can have a bigger impact than an overly complicated palette.

MAXX PRO TIP

Preparation makes the finish.

Before buying paint, photograph the room and look at it from the doorway. It is often easier to identify the wall that should change when you view the entire space at once.

QUICK SUMMARY

Key takeaways

  • Start with one visual change.
  • Use coordinated shades.
  • Consider the ceiling.
  • Keep accent colours intentional.

Let that goal guide the colour direction. 03 · Create a focal pointAn accent wall, architectural feature or carefully selected colour can give the eye a natural point of focus without overwhelming the room. 04 · Coordinate the surroundingsPaint works alongside furniture, flooring, curtains, lighting and décor.
